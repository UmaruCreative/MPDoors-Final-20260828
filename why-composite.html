Why MP Doors

The failure is never the surface. It is the edge.

A fiberglass panel on a 100% waterproof composite frame, sealed on six sides, certified across all four United States climate zones.

  1. MP Doors
  2. Why MP Doors

The premise

We did not add a coating. We removed the reason a door needs one.

A door does not fail in the showroom. It fails in the fourth winter, at the joint, where water was patient.

Most exterior doors are built from a material that wants water, then defended with a finish that is asked to keep water out for twenty-five years. MP Doors starts from the other end: a frame and edging that cannot absorb water at all, so the finish is asked only to look good.

The constant

Ice, heat and salt cannot enter a sealed edge.

The same frame, in the Northern freeze-thaw and in Florida salt air. No regional variant, no compromise line.

One door · four zones

Each zone has its own violence

ENERGY STAR certifies windows and doors across four United States climate zones. Most manufacturers meet them with regional variants. MP Doors meets all four with one composite door.

Northern

Freeze–thaw

Water enters a joint as liquid and leaves as ice nine percent larger, several hundred times a season. A composite frame cannot take the water in.

North Central

Thermal cycling

A single year spans a hundred-degree swing. Composite moves less, and it moves the same in July and January.

South Central

Sustained UV

Heat is the baseline, not an event. The composite core holds its dimension under sustained temperature.

Southern

Salt and storm

Permanent humidity, salt air and storm-borne debris. The composite frame does not corrode or rot, and the Florida impact series is missile-rated to code.
