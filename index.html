The constant

Sealed on six sides,
as one closed body

HydroShield™ treats the whole door as a single surface.

Face and reverse, both stiles, the top rail and the bottom rail. Six sides, sealed as one continuous barrier around the whole body of the door.

It is why the frame is 100% waterproof by construction rather than by coating, and why looking after it comes down to mild soap, water and a soft cloth.
